# Colours

> The foundation's palette: four colours, with their roles and values for screen and print.

The palette is short: a black background, a red accent, white for text, and printing black for light backgrounds.

## Palette
| Colour | Role | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Black | Leading background | #000000 | 0 0 0 | C60 M40 Y40 K100 (rich black, for large areas) | Black 6 C |
| Red | Leading colour | #D91919 | 217 25 25 | C0 M95 Y100 K0 | 485 C |
| Printing black | Alternative colour: contrast on a light background | #231F20 | 35 31 32 | C0 M0 Y0 K100 | Neutral Black C |
| White | Text and the sign on a dark background | #FFFFFF | 255 255 255 | C0 M0 Y0 K0 | No ink (the colour of the paper) |

## Where each colour goes
- **Red #D91919** is the colour of the red bands in the negative and positive variants of the logo, and the background of the variant on the brand colour.
- **Printing black #231F20** is the dark colour of the positive variant on light backgrounds, and the only colour of the monochrome variant.
- **White** is the light colour of the negative variant on dark backgrounds, and the only colour of the variant on brand red.
- **Black #000000** is the leading background. Its CMYK value is a rich black, meant for large areas.

See [Colour variants](./logo.md#colour-variants) for the variants themselves.
