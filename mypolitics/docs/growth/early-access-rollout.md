# Early access rollout

> Returning takers get a high-traffic quiz a couple of hours before everyone else.

**Decision:** **⚪ idea**

## Context
Raised by Oskar Barcz on Discord on 2026-10-06, together with [profile badges](./profile-badges.md). There is no design and no product documentation for it yet.

The idea as proposed:

- **A staged launch** - when an election quiz goes live, or any quiz where we expect a very large audience, it does not open to everyone at once.
- **Returning takers first** - people who have used the platform before, for example those holding three badges, get in about two hours ahead of the public.
- **The point is the load** - the early group takes the first wave, so the peak is spread instead of arriving in one moment.

It depends on [profile badges](./profile-badges.md), or on some other way of telling a returning taker from a new one, and so on [accounts](../platform/accounts.md).

## Opportunity
- **A flatter peak** - election quizzes are where we expect the heaviest traffic, and part of it moves to a window we choose.
- **A rehearsal with real users** - two hours with a smaller, friendlier audience is a chance to catch what testing missed before the public arrives.
- **Loyalty gets something real** - early access is a reward that costs us nothing to give.
- **It gives badges a purpose** - a collection that unlocks something is worth more than a collection that is only looked at.
- **A launch becomes an event** - "opens to everyone at 18:00" is something to announce and wait for.

## Risk
- **It gates a quiz behind an account** - [accounts](../platform/accounts.md) say an account never gates taking a quiz, and for two hours this one would.
- **The early group is not the public** - returning takers are more engaged and may lean differently, so anything shown from early data - statistics, rarity, comparisons - starts skewed.
- **Results leak before launch** - screenshots and shared cards from the first two hours reach the public before the quiz does, and partners who planned a coordinated premiere are overtaken.
- **Two hours may not move the load** - if the early group is small, the public peak is as high as before and we have added a mechanism for nothing.
- **It has to be enforced somewhere** - access control on a quiz is new, and on the day of the highest traffic it is one more thing that can fail.
- **It needs badges and accounts first** - neither is designed, so this cannot be built before both are.
