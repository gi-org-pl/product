# Legacy content migration

> Moving the old videos and articles from the old services to the new one, so the heritage of myPolitics is kept.

**Decision:** **⚪ idea**

## Context
Raised by the owner in October 2026 as an initiative to move all old content - videos and articles - from the old services to the new one, to preserve the heritage of myPolitics. There is no design and no product documentation for it yet.

What the documents and the repository say about the old services and their content, and nothing more:

- **The old stack exists** - [quiz migration](../platform/quiz-migration.md) says the old quizzes still run on the old stack, which is expensive to keep up, and that the goal is to turn it off.
- **It holds more than quizzes** - the same file says the old stack holds community quizzes from the 2023 editor, that links people have shared point at results on it, and that it is the only copy of anything never migrated.
- **Videos and articles are not mentioned** - a search of the product docs for old content, videos, articles, blogs and archives finds nothing about them. The documents do not say which old services there are, where the videos and articles live, how many there are or who owns them.
- **The media module is about new content** - [news ingestion](../modules/media/aggregation/news-ingestion.md) pulls in articles from outside, and the distribution docs cover publishing from now on. Neither keeps what was published before.

So the starting point is an unknown, not an inventory. The first step of such an initiative would be to list what exists.

## Opportunity
- **The track record is the asset** - [how](../concept/how.md) says myPolitics has run since 2018, and the content from those years is the evidence of it.
- **It supports the credibility argument** - being able to point to what was said and published over the years is something a newer competitor cannot do.
- **It goes with switching off the old stack** - [quiz migration](../platform/quiz-migration.md) says the old stack is only worth turning off once nothing is left on it, and this would be a part of "nothing".
- **Old content can feed new places** - an article or a video from the archive is material for the social and media work the [media module](../modules/media/README.md) describes.

## Risk
What the documents say:

- **Switching off is irreversible** - the old stack is the only copy of whatever was not moved, see [quiz migration](../platform/quiz-migration.md). Videos and articles stored there would be lost with it unless they are counted first.
- **Shared links are a promise** - the same file says breaking a result someone posted years ago is a visible failure. Links to old articles and videos would be the same.
- **Migration competes with everything** - it produces no new features, so it is the work that gets postponed.
- **Nothing in the docs gives the content a home** - the [modules](../modules/README.md) are quiz, data, polls and media, and none of them is an archive.
- **Credibility is the one asset that cannot be rebuilt** - [principles](../concept/principles.md) say nothing ships that we could not defend publicly, and old content was written before these principles were.

Our reading, not stated in the documents:

- **Rights are unknown** - who owns a video, whether it contains third-party material and whether an article's author agreed to its reuse are all open.
- **Old content can carry personal data** - names, comments and faces of people who did not agree to be on a new service.
- **Old content can be out of date** - an article about an election long past needs to be marked with its date and context rather than presented as current.
- **It may be two different jobs** - preserving (a copy that is kept) and publishing (a place where people find it) need different amounts of work, and the idea does not say which is meant.
- **It needs a decision on scope** - "all" content is the wording of the idea, and the docs give no way to know how large that is.
