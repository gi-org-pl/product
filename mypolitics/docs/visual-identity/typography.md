## Typeface
The typeface of myPolitics is **Poppins**. It covers the full Polish alphabet:

```
AĄBCĆDEĘFGHIJKLŁMNŃOÓPQRSŚTUVWXYZŹŻ
aąbcćdeęfghijklłmnńoópqrsśtuvwxyzźż
0123456789 !?&@%
```

Poppins is available from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

## Weights
Three weights are used, each with one role.

| Weight | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Bold | 700 | Titles and headings |
| Medium | 500 | Labels, captions, buttons and navigation |
| Regular | 400 | Body text and descriptions |
