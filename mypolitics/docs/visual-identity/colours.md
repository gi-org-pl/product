## Roles
- **Dark teal #003642** - the lettering of the positive version and the colour of the monochrome version.
- **Interface teal #004554** - the main colour of the app's interface.
- **The three blues** - the bars of the logomark in the universal family, from top to bottom: dark blue, blue, light blue.

How the bars contrast with each background is in [Usage](./usage.md#contrast).
