# Discovery

> Where community quizzes surface and how they are ordered.

**Decision:** **⚪ idea**

## Context
Surfaces:
- a browsable community catalog alongside our own quizzes
- the [follow-up recommendation](../results/follow-up-recommendation.md) after a finished result
- the creator's own share links, which work regardless of ranking

Ordering comes from [quality score](./quality-score.md), narrowed by category and tags. Official and community quizzes are always visually distinct.

## Opportunity
- The audience is the thing we actually bring to creators - discovery is how it reaches them.
- A finished result is the best moment to start another quiz: it raises quizzes per session and gives new creators their first users.
- Non-political community quizzes broaden the entry points without touching the official catalog, and are the natural feed into [myQuizzes](../myquizzes/README.md).

## Risk
- Mixing community and official content muddies what carries our editorial name; labelling has to be unmistakable.
- A large catalog with weak taxonomy is worse than a small one.
- Recommending an unrelated quiz right after a political result cheapens the result screen.

## Decision
Idea. Taxonomy - fixed categories, free tags, or both - is unresolved and blocks the catalog design.
